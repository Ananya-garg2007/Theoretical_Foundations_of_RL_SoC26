# Week 1 Intuition

Reinforcement Learning is about learning from experience.

Instead of being told what is correct, the agent figures things out by interacting with the environment.

## Key Idea

You don’t know the best action initially.

You:
- try things
- observe outcomes
- improve decisions

## Exploration vs Exploitation

This is like:

- Studying from a known method → exploitation  
- Trying a new study method → exploration  

Balancing both leads to better results.

## Big Picture

RL is not about immediate success — it’s about **long-term improvement**.
